## Document governance

Before any policy is used operationally, the organisation should:

1.  Replace generic wording with requirements that reflect its business
    model, technology environment, risk profile, and applicable legal
    obligations.
2.  Assign a policy owner and obtain review from relevant stakeholders,
    including Information Security, IT, Legal, Compliance, Human
    Resources, and business management where appropriate.
3.  Record the document version, approval authority, approval date,
    effective date, and next review date.
4.  Communicate approved requirements to affected personnel and retain
    evidence of acknowledgement or training where appropriate.
5.  Review the policy at planned intervals and after significant
    organisational, regulatory, technology, or security changes.

## Status and use

The documents in this repository are portfolio examples and working
templates. They are not evidence that an organisation has approved,
implemented, or complied with the requirements. Do not treat them as
approved policies until they have been tailored, reviewed, authorised,
communicated, and implemented by the relevant organisation.
